## Overview

{{APP_NAME}} ("the Game") is a mobile game. We designed it to collect as little
as possible. This policy explains what is collected, by whom, and what choices
you have.

## Information we collect

**From you, directly:** almost nothing. If you email us for support at
{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use
it only to answer you.

**The Game itself:** no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no servers of ours
storing your personal data. Your high scores and settings stay on your device.

**Automatically, through advertising:** the free version of the Game shows ads
served by Google AdMob. AdMob and Google may collect:

- Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
- Device type, operating system version, and general device information
- Approximate location (derived from IP address)
- App interaction data (which ads were shown or tapped) for ad measurement

This is governed by Google's own policies:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## How we use information

- To show ads in the free version (that's how the free version stays free)
- To respond to support emails
- To comply with legal obligations

## Data sharing

We share data only as described above: with Google/AdMob for advertising, and
with authorities if required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

## Your choices

- **Personalized ads:** opt out in your device settings
  (iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking; Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
- **Remove ads entirely:** buy the Remove Ads in-app purchase ($2.99) inside
  the Game — no more AdMob data collection from the Game after that.
- **Support emails:** email us and ask; we'll delete your message.

## Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
data from children under 13.
