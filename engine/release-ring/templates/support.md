## Frequently asked questions

### How do I fly?
Drag anywhere on the screen — your plane follows your finger. Double-tap to
barrel-roll through danger. Your guns fire automatically.

### How do I remove the ads?
Open the Game, go to Settings → Remove Ads ($2.99, one-time purchase).
No subscription, no tricks.

### I bought Remove Ads but still see ads — help?
Tap Settings → **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you're signed in with the same
Apple ID / Google account you bought it with. Still stuck? Email us.

### The game runs slowly on my phone.
Close background apps and try again. The Game is tuned for devices from the
last several years; very old phones may struggle with the later sorties.

### I found a bug!
We want to hear about it: email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with your device model and
what happened. Screenshots help a lot.
